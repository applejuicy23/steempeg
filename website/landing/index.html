Steempeg

A fast, hardware-accelerated renderer for Steam Game Recording clips. Recover broken recordings, trim with precision, and export in one click.

Download Getting Started Docs

Free & open source · GitHub

Clips, not folders

Browse Steam recordings as cards. Filter by game, health, and duration. Repair broken block-spliced clips.

Trim & mark

Timeline with sniper thumbs, custom markers, theatre and immersive fullscreen — Desktop or Portable shell.

Queue & export

NVENC or CPU. H.264 through AV1 and VP9. Batch queue that survives restarts, with honest bitrate caps.

Ready when you are

Pick the zip for your platform, extract, and run. No installer.
